Experience

My experience includes working with IMUs, time-of-flight sensors, pulsed coherent radar (PCR), and environmental sensors, supporting both hardware integration and software-side data processing. This includes a Space Lab project involving the design and validation of a scatter detector chamber and custom PCB to measure CO₂ concentration in ambient air, integrated into a high-altitude balloon (HAB) system and developed under strict power, mass, and environmental constraints.

I also completed a semester-long research project on Fourier series, focused on signal decomposition and frequency-domain behavior in physical measurement systems, strengthening my foundation in applied signal processing.

As part of my capstone project, I worked on a multi-sensor system focused on long-term monitoring of small shifts in a home’s foundation, with an emphasis on data reliability through system integration, testing, and analysis. I also served in a project leadership role, coordinating testing efforts, documentation, and cross-subsystem communication across a multi-disciplinary team.

In addition, I’ve applied machine learning techniques in Python to IMU and PCR signal processing tasks, supporting feature extraction, noise characterization, and data-driven analysis alongside traditional filtering methods.

About Me

I began my career in hairstyling at 17, working for LoLa Beauty and specializing in wedding styling. Thirteen years later, I’m still with the company. I’ve always loved playing a small role in making someone’s most important day feel even more special. That work taught me responsibility early, how to manage pressure, and how to show up fully for other people.

Over time, though, I realized hairstyling wouldn’t be my forever path. I decided to return to school as a non-traditional student, starting at community college without a clear direction — just a desire to challenge myself and find something mentally engaging. After taking business calculus, I discovered that my favorite classes were math-based. That curiosity led me to research fields that would combine complexity, problem-solving, and meaningful contribution. Electrical Engineering felt like the right fit.

Balancing full-time work while paying for school out of pocket has been one of the toughest and most rewarding experiences of my life. I’m proud to say I made it through without student loans. The journey has strengthened my discipline and reinforced that I can commit fully to long-term goals.

Outside of work and school, I’ve traveled to 12 countries and plan to keep exploring. I love going to the Renaissance festival each year (The Lord of the Rings trilogy is easily in my top ten), paddle boarding, painting, lifting weights, and taking a variety of dance classes. I’m someone who enjoys growth — whether that’s intellectual, physical, or creative.
