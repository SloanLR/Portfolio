HAB Scatter Detector Payload

Custom PCB-based photodiode scatter detector designed for CO₂ sensing in a high-altitude balloon (HAB) system, integrating analog signal conditioning, embedded processing, and mechanical enclosure design.

Project Overview

This project focused on the design and validation of a compact scatter detector system for measuring atmospheric CO₂ concentration in a high-altitude balloon (HAB) environment. The system integrates a photodiode-based sensing mechanism with an analog front-end, filtering stages, and digital acquisition via a microcontroller.

The design process included schematic development, PCB layout, analog signal conditioning using a transimpedance amplifier (TIA), and mechanical enclosure modeling for optical isolation. Emphasis was placed on signal integrity, noise reduction, and reliable data acquisition under constrained environmental conditions.

Tools: KiCad, SolidWorks, LTSpice, Arduino IDE

Domain: Embedded Systems, PCB Design, Analog Signal Processing

Focus: Photodiode Sensing, TIA Design, Signal Filtering, Data Acquisition

System Architecture & Design

The system was designed as a modular sensing pipeline converting optical scatter into a measurable electrical signal. The architecture integrates a photodiode sensing interface, analog signal conditioning, filtering, and ADC-based digitization.

PCB Design & Integration

Designed for low-noise analog acquisition with careful grounding, routing, and component placement.

  • ADS1115 ADC for high-resolution measurement
  • I²C communication with processor
  • Decoupling and grounding optimized

Analog Front-End

  • OPA320 TIA for current-to-voltage conversion
  • 2-pole LPF for noise suppression
  • Improved ADC stability
