Nestor Guillen

Néstor D. Guillen is an Associate Professor of Mathematics at Texas State University whose research centers on partial differential equations, nonlocal operators, and nonlinear analysis. His work advances theoretical understanding of elliptic and integro-differential equations, with applications to mathematical physics and applied analysis, supported in part by competitive research funding including an NSF CAREER award.
